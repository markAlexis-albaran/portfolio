# Site Brief

Baseline: the existing Framer portfolio at https://markalexis.framer.website. Match its structure, content and feel as closely as possible unless a rule below says otherwise. Use placeholders for anything not yet decided (images, case study content).

## Structure
- Multi-page: a home page, an About page, and one page per case study.
- The home page shows all five projects. Each card links to its own case study page (e.g. `projects/tvguideplus.html`). Each case study has an "← All projects" link at the top and again at the bottom, right before the contact section, both going back to the projects on the home page (`index.html#work`), so nobody has to scroll up or use Home to get back.
- Projects page: hidden for now. `projects.html` stays in the folder but nothing links to it, and search engines are asked to skip it. Bring it back (nav link and back links) once there are more than five projects.
- About page: yes
- A small "built with Claude ;-)", led by a small drawn spark (eight rays, in the text color, a nod to Claude's mark but not the official logo) (written exactly like that, owner's request) in grey mono directly under the nav name on screens 810px and wider, and as the last line of the phone menu. Plain text, not a link.
- Name in the nav: "markAlexis-Albaran" at every screen size, with "built with Claude ;-)" under it (9px on phones, 11px on larger screens; the "mA" mark is no longer used). On phones the nav is a floating rounded bar inset from the edges, with a subtle two-line menu button that opens a small floating panel (Home, About, Dark mode switch); it closes on Escape, a tap outside, or choosing a link. Desktop keeps the full nav, no menu button. Screen readers hear "Mark Alexis Albaran, home".
- Navigation: top bar with Home, About, then a "Dark mode" toggle switch. Light mode is the default. Visible on every page.
- Floating "Contact" button at the bottom right of every page (blue accent, white mono text), replacing the old Contact nav link. It scrolls to the contact section at the bottom of the current page (no mailto links anywhere: they open a mail app, which felt jarring) and hides while that section is on screen. On phones it waits until the home page's centered "Scroll down" button has hidden, so the two never overlap.

## Case Study Display
- Index: cards with a thumbnail image, year, category and project name. Placeholder images until real ones exist.
- Projects (visible h2 "Projects", no divider line before the contact section): the five case studies as a stack of overlapping folders, not a grid of cards. The folders are drawn like a wireframe: the page color with a thin outline (black at 25% in light mode, white at 28% in dark), with an outlined bookmark-style tab on each folder's left edge, text running bottom to top like a book spine (no dot). Only the folder on top shows its full name on the tab ("01 SpringIn"); the others show their number, and covered tabs peek out 12px above the next one in the stack (screen readers still get every name). Inside: category · year, then the result as the title (big number plus its line, e.g. "3 problem frames before the right one"), then the one-line description. The project name appears only on the tab. Folders have no hover effects. The open folder shows its brand logo (the loading-screen image) on the right, as a small square in the top-right corner on phones. The logo has no animation of its own: it's revealed with the rest of the folder's content as the folder unfolds, inside a thin outline. The projects are a flight through the work (after vadimcarazan.com; the spiral version was tried and dropped): the section pins under the nav, a full screen tall, and scrolling moves the camera forward, about four fifths of a screen of scrolling per project. Each project's loading-screen card (one flat panel, thin outline in the text color) appears in the distance as an empty outlined box like the small squares around it, at its own spot a little away from the middle (spread around it, never twice in a row on the same side); it glides to the center as it comes closer, and the image fills the box once the card before it has gone; it then settles centered above the caption, then bursts past and fades; small outlined squares drift through the depth around it. Only one card image shows at a time; the caption switches to the next project as its image fills in. The section opens on "Problems I've untangled." in large light type in the middle (no subtitle) with no card in view; the line fades out while the first card appears in the distance, then the first project's name fades in. When the scrolling stops, the cards drift gently like feathers (a slow bob, sway and tilt). The project in focus is named in the middle of the view (only its name and category · year; no project numbers) with a "Read case study →" button under it. Clicking a card also opens its case study. Screen readers get the plain project list; the 3D view is hidden from them. The front project's result (big light number plus its line) and its one-line description float on the left of the view, above a thin divider, and drift with the cards; they fade out and back in when the project changes. On phones they sit at the bottom of the view, under the caption. After the last project the section runs on a little: the last card bursts past and fades while the caption and the facts drift up and fade at the same time; the contact section follows right after, with no empty pause (the closing takes less than half a screen of scrolling). The visible "Projects" heading above the section is hidden while the orbit runs (screen readers still get it). The project at the front is named in the middle of the view (only its name and category · year; no project numbers) with a "Read case study →" button under it; cards pass in front of the caption as they move. Clicking a card also opens its case study. Screen readers get the plain project list; the 3D view is hidden from them. With reduced motion on, the cards are a plain list with everything shown. The open folder gets a tab in its project's dark brand color with white text (SpringIn #1F5E55, tvguideplus #121585, Columna #C4512A, yumm #2E7D72, pflegePro #D93A30; each at least 4.5:1 against white, checked with axe in both modes) and a full-strength outline (black in light mode, white in dark); the other folders keep the lighter outline. Collapsed folders show only their tab and category · year; the result, description and "Read case study" fold away and unfold smoothly when the folder opens (screen readers still get all of it). The open card always shows "Read case study →". 
- Inside case study: sections with an anchor menu at the top that jumps to each section.
- Walkthrough section (labeled "Walkthrough", not "Prototype", because it plays on its own): the last section of every case study (after Outcome, before the closing "All projects" link), listed in the anchor menu. ## References
- Like: the Framer portfolio (markalexis.framer.website) because it is the owner's own baseline: keep its structure, content, spacing and feel as much as possible.
- Dislike: dull, static sites with nothing moving, and sites where motion or layout makes it hard to find the work or get around.

## Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)
- White focus outline on every focusable element (the blue accent is only 2.5:1 on black).
- A "Skip to content" link first on every page, pointing to `<main id="main">`.
- No skipped heading levels: the home page has a hidden "Projects" h2 above the cards.
- Nothing moves for more than 5 seconds without a way to pause it.

## Do NOT generate
- Animations that delay or block reading the content or using the navigation.
- Hidden or hard-to-find navigation (no hamburger-only menu on desktop, no nav that disappears).
- A single long page holding every project.
- Any content, project or page not listed above.
